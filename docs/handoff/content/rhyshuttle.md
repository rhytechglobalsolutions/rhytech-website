## What is RhyShuttle?

RhyShuttle is Rhytech's tournament software for badminton clubs. It takes your club from WhatsApp chaos to professional tournaments — live scoring, auto-generated brackets, player stats and rankings, session RSVP, and a venue TV display.

## How it helps you

For clubs — run professional tournaments with no spreadsheets; fixtures, brackets, points tables and rankings update themselves. For players — follow every rally live, track your stats and climb the rankings. For organizers — replace WhatsApp polls with one-tap session RSVP, and put the live bracket on the venue TV for everyone to see.

## Challenges we fix

Scores and schedules buried in WhatsApp group chaos; hours wasted hand-drawing knockout brackets and updating points tables; no player history, stats or rankings across tournaments; members at home unable to follow live matches; session attendance guesswork every week.
