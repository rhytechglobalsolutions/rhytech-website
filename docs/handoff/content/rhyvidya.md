## What is RhyVidya?

RhyVidya is Rhytech's online teaching platform that brings expert teachers from India to students anywhere in the world, live. Starting with Hindi language learning, students join interactive live classes, practice with a free video library, and progress through structured stages.

## How it helps you

For parents abroad — your child learns their mother tongue from qualified native teachers, in structured stages, scheduled around your life. For learners — join from anywhere in the world and learn at your own pace, from Beginner to Advanced. Live interaction with a real teacher, easy online booking, and WhatsApp support throughout.

## Challenges we fix

No access to qualified native-language teachers outside India; unstructured learning from random videos with no progression path; scheduling chaos across time zones; no easy way for parents to track a child's progress.
